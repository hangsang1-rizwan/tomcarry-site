Product Overview:

Keep your little girl cosy and stylish this winter with this short-length denim jacket designed especially for infants and toddlers. Featuring a durable denim outer shell, a warm fleece lining throughout, and a large, plush fur (or faux-fur) collar, this jacket blends rugged charm with soft, winter-ready comfort.

Key Features:

Outer fabric: Classic denim (100% cotton or cotton-rich blend) giving structure and style, ideal for casual outings and layering.

Lining & insulation: Full inner lining made of soft fleece (or sherpa) for warmth and comfort, helping to trap heat in chilly conditions. According to parents’ guides, fleece-lined interiors significantly boost warmth without excessive bulk.

Large fur collar: A standout oversized collar trimmed in fur (real fur or high-quality faux fur) adds a luxe touch and extra neck warmth.

Short/hip length cut: Tailored to infant/toddler ages—shorter than a full-length coat so it’s practical for sitting, crawling (for infants), and general mobility.

Front closure: Button or snap closure typical of denim-style jackets; may include chest pockets and side welt pockets for detail.

Cuffs & hem: Simple hem and cuff finish consistent with denim jackets, allowing layering of long-sleeve tops underneath.

Sizes: Infant (e.g., 12-24 months) and toddler (2-4 yrs, 3-6 yrs) ranges.

Care instructions: Remove or treat fur collar per label; machine wash cold (if permitted) or hand wash; line dry; avoid harsh bleaches to maintain fur texture and fleece lining.

Why It Works:

Combines the casual cool-factor of denim with winter-appropriate warmth from fleece and fur.

Fur collar adds visual interest and added warmth at the neck—especially useful for breezy winter days.

Fleece interior ensures internal comfort — according to guides, warmth and appropriate insulation are key factors for kids’ winter outerwear.

Styling Tips:

Pair the jacket with a pastel knit dress and wool tights for a cute winter outing look.

For a more laid-back style, combine with dark leggings and boots—leaving the fur collar unzipped or folded down gives a more casual vibe.

Use a beanie matching the fur colour for cohesion and extra warmth around ears.

Sizing & Fit Notes:

Ensure there’s enough room for a layer underneath (long-sleeve shirt or light sweater).

Because toddlers grow fast, consider sizing up one to maximise wear time.

Make sure cuffs are not too tight — good mobility matters since children move a lot.

For infants (who may crawl or sit more), the short length ensures the jacket doesn’t interfere with movement.

Export & Quality Notes (if for wholesale/export use):

Select quality hardware (metal snaps/buttons) and sturdy denim fabric to hold up in transit and retail handling.

Ensure fur collar is well attached (secure stitching) and possibly detachable for easier cleaning and variation in look.

Provide clear size chart (especially for export to international markets) with chest, length and sleeve measurements.

Packaging: folded with collar protected, tag clearly indicating “fleece-lined” and “large fur collar” to communicate value.
