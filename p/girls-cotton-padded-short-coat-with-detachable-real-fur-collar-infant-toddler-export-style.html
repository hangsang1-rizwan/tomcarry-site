Full Product Description

Product Overview:

This charming short-length girls’ coat is designed for the little ones (infants to toddlers) who deserve both warmth and style. With a high-quality cotton exterior, comfortable padded insulation, and a luxurious detachable real-fur collar, this coat brings together export-level finishing, everyday practicality and elegant details.

Key Features:

Outer fabric: Premium cotton (or cotton-rich blend) shell that offers a soft, natural feel and good breathability — ideal for delicate skin.

Padding/Insulation: Medium-weight padded cotton-fill (or cotton-blend fill) insulation that keeps the child warm without heavy bulk — balancing mobility with warmth.

Length & cut: Short (hip-length) design — perfect for toddlers and infants; allows natural movement, easy sitting/crawling, while still offering good coverage.

Detachable real fur collar: A removable fur trim around the collar adds a luxurious “export style” touch. The collar snaps or zips off easily for washing or for a simpler look.

Front closure: Smooth front zip with fabric placket and snap buttons (or press-studs) for extra wind protection and neat finish.

Cuffs & hem: Elastic or softly gathered cuffs to keep out cold air; hem sits at a flattering short length, sometimes with a gentle flare for ease of movement.

Lining: Smooth soft lining (often polyester or cotton-blend) for comfort and ease of dressing; may include a small internal loop for hanging.

Colours & design: Typically available in classic export-style colours — soft pastels (pink, lavender, mint), neutrals (cream, beige, navy) and often minimal embellishments so it pairs easily with other outfits.

Sizing: Infant sizes (e.g., 0-2 yrs) and toddler sizes (e.g., 2-4 yrs, 3-6 yrs) — with slight room for layering underneath.

Care instructions: Recommend removing the fur collar before washing; machine wash at gentle cycle (or hand wash) for the outer shell; line dry. Fur trim may require separate care or dry cleaning depending on material.

Export-quality finishing: Clean stitching, quality zippers and hardware, neatly attached fur and linings, making this suitable for international markets and gift-worthy occasions.

Why You’ll Love It:

It merges functionality — keeping your little girl warm and comfortable — with style-forward design details (real fur collar, cotton shell).

The short length makes it versatile for everyday wear, visits, and family outings.

The detachable collar means you get two looks in one: a refined look for special occasions and a simpler version for everyday.

The natural cotton exterior is ideal for sensitive skin (infants/toddlers) and for those who prefer more natural fabric choices.

Styling Tips:

Pair with leggings or slim-fit pants and ankle boots for a chic winter look.

Remove the fur collar for a more casual, everyday vibe and let the coat blend seamlessly with smart-casual outfits.

Add a knit hat or ear-muff set in a matching or contrasting colour for extra warmth and cuteness.

Important Sizing & Fit Notes:

Measure the child’s height and chest circumference and allow room for layering.

For babies/toddlers who grow quickly, consider sizing up one size for longer wearable time.

Ensure the cuffs and hem are not too tight – comfortable movement matters.
